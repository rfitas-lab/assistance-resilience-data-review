# Focal systematic core protocol

## Review question
Which empirical educational-AI studies measure human capability beyond the assisted episode through independent performance, delayed retention, transfer, persistence/effort, or behavior under incorrect/degraded AI?

## Eligibility
Include empirical behavioral studies in educational or learning settings that expose learners to AI assistance and contain at least one capability outcome that is independent of the target assistance, delayed, transfer-oriented, persistence/effort-related, or an incorrect/degraded-AI stress test. Randomized, quasi-experimental, longitudinal, and controlled laboratory designs are eligible.

Exclude secondary reviews from the focal denominator; self-report-only, perception-only, qualitative-only, policy/conceptual, adoption, and measurement-development reports without a behavioral retained/stress-tested capability outcome. Secondary/contextual reports may still inform the integrative outer review.

## Coding
For included reports code design/setting, sample, assistance architecture, AI-on outcome where available, independent/AI-off outcome, delay, transfer, incorrect-AI/calibration, persistence/effort, subgroup/equity information, and publication status. The focal core is a structured audit of the construct-relevant literature and is not claimed to be an exhaustive census of every educational-AI report.
